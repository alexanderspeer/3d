Summary:
Launch Pad for Model rockets.  Prints with no supports and legs friction fit into base.  Print a base and 3 legs.Recommended clear out center hole with a 3.5 mm drill bit for a easy fit of 1/8 inch rod and using a metal disc (or small pizza pan for an inexpensive metal disc as shown in the picture) to protect the plastic parts from rocket blast.
